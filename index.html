Luxury Travel · Jaipur, Rajasthan · India
India Client
01 — The Challenge

A premium, high-touch travel business with strong on-ground service but limited digital visibility for the luxury audience it was built for.

02 — The Strategy

Reposition the brand around exclusivity and craftsmanship, with SEO and Instagram built specifically for a luxury, intent-driven audience.

03 — The Execution

Brand positioning refresh, an Aadyaa Growth Calendar for content and SEO, and a special showcase for flagship tour experiences.

04 — The Growth

Reach, search visibility and enquiry growth tracked from campaign launch.

05 — The Report

Monthly reporting against reach, search visibility and lead enquiries, rolled into a yearly growth summary.

06 — The Next Step

Expanding SEO into international luxury-travel search terms and layering in performance campaigns.
